# Screepub 0.9.0

**Telling us about a device now reaches the internet, and only when you press the button.** Sending starts right where your book appears, and a script PDF can become a script you edit again.

## Convert, then send, on one page

- **Your book and your reader, side by side.** The Send tab is gone.
  When a script converts, its first page appears beside your reader,
  asked once and remembered. A plugged-in Kindle gets one button that
  copies the right file.
- **Eject your reader after a copy.** After copying over USB, the
  button becomes **Eject Kindle**, so you can unplug safely without a
  trip to the Finder.
- **Get the script back to edit.** Under **Other ways**, **Save a file**
  now offers FDX for Final Draft and most screenwriting apps, beside
  Fountain. It is rebuilt from the PDF's pages, so revision colors and
  exact page breaks stay behind.
- **The first page types itself out while you wait.** It is on by
  default. Turn off **Animate the page** in Settings if you'd rather not
  watch. **Typewriter sounds** adds key clicks, and starts off.

## Good to know

- **My device isn't here** and the **It worked** and **It did not work**
  answers send what you typed or chose, the app version and your system
  to a service that keeps only counts and the day.
- Eject on Windows and Linux, and FDX files in screenwriting apps,
  haven't been tried on a real reader or app yet.
