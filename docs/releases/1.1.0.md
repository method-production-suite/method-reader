# Method Reader 1.1.0

**Newer Kindles take your scripts over USB, and scripts that tripped Method Reader up now come through as the writer laid them out.**

## More from every script

- **Copy to a newer Kindle over USB (beta).** Kindles from late 2024 on connect the way a phone does, so until now they could only take books by email. On a Mac, plug one in and choose Copy to Kindle.
- **Bring an .osf as well.** A script in the Open Screenplay Format becomes a book, just as a PDF or a .fountain does.
- **Method Reader's own Kindle file is no longer beta.** A newer Kindle opened it with every scene in its contents and every name beside its lines.

## Fixed

- **Names left alone at the foot of a Kindle page.** The printed page number pulled a name away from its lines. It now sits on the nearest description, usually a few lines on, and Go to page still works.
- **A reader's name stamped across every page.** A diagonal watermark broke speeches in half and cut names off from their lines. It is now left out.
- **Unusual layouts.** Parentheticals that wrap or sit further right, a name straight after side-by-side dialogue, and scripts typed with a word processor's margins all read correctly now.

## Good to know

- Copying to a newer Kindle over USB is on the Mac for now. On Windows and Linux those Kindles still take books by email.
- A script converted before this release keeps its old reading. Convert it again to pick up the fixes.
- While a book copies to a newer Kindle, Photos and Image Capture cannot import from a camera or phone. Method Reader turns that back on when the copy ends.
