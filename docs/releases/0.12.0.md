# Screepub 0.12.0

Screepub is rebuilt from the ground up as one native program, and your scripts come out exactly as they did before.

## A new Screepub, the same books

- **Every book is the same as before.** The new Screepub was checked
  against the old one on every test script and on real scripts, and the
  books match. Your settings, your library and your reader carry over.
- **Read it or edit it.** After a script converts, choose **Read it** to
  send it to your reader, or **Edit it** to save it for Final Draft
  (beta) or for Highland, Fade In and other Fountain apps, without
  hunting through **Other ways**.
- **A new Linux download.** An AppImage now sits beside the Debian and
  Fedora packages.

## Good to know

- The first time it opens, Screepub may ask once more whether to check
  for updates.
- The Windows and Linux downloads are checked automatically on every
  build but haven't been installed by a person yet. Tell us how it goes.
