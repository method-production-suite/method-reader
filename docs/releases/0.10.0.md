# Screepub 0.10.0

**Screepub now needs a Mac with Apple silicon: on an Intel Mac, 0.9.0 is the last version, and it keeps working.** The pictures in your script now come through into the book.

## Your script's pictures, in the book

- **Storyboards and photos stay where they sat.** Panels, reference
  photos and lookbook pages used to be dropped, leaving only the text
  around them. Now they appear in place. Turn off **Pictures from the
  script** in Settings for a smaller file.
- **A designed title page becomes the cover.** A full-page title card at
  the front now shows as the book's cover in your library, instead of a
  plain one. Turn off **Title card as the cover** to keep it as the first
  page.
- **Saved Fountain files are clean.** A Fountain file you saved for a
  screenwriting app used to carry Screepub's own hidden notes. Now it
  leaves them behind.

## Good to know

- Pictures, the cover, FDX files, Eject on Windows and Linux, and the
  device report buttons are marked Beta: they work in our tests, but
  haven't been tried widely yet. Tell us how it goes.
- Most e-ink screens are greyscale, so a color picture reads as black and
  white. The Kindle file Screepub makes without Calibre leaves pictures out
  for now.
- On an Intel Mac with the older Mac app, choose Not Now when this update
  is offered: it will not open there. Download Screepub 0.9.0 from its
  release page instead.
