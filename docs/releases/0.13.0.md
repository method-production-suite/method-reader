# Method Reader 0.13.0

**Method Reader now checks for updates on its own, and you can turn that off in the Help menu.** Screepub is now Method Reader, part of the Method Production Suite, and your books come out exactly as before.

## A new name, the same books

- **Your settings and library move over.** The first time it opens,
  Method Reader moves your settings into its own folder, and a library in
  the usual place moves to Documents, under Method Reader. A folder you
  chose yourself stays where it is. Nothing is converted again.
- **A calmer window.** It now looks like the other Method apps: your
  system's own font, plain buttons, and a normal title bar. The script
  itself keeps its typewriter font.
- **Everything else is in the Help menu.** About, keyboard shortcuts,
  What's New, the licences, and Report a Bug.

## Updates take care of themselves

- **Updates arrive on their own.** On a Mac, two minutes after it opens
  and every six hours after that, Method Reader asks GitHub whether there is a new
  version, downloads it, and says Update ready. If you said no to
  updates before, it stays off. Change it with **Check for Updates
  Automatically** in the Help menu.

## Good to know

- On a Mac, the app keeps the name Screepub in your Applications folder
  until the next update renames it. It opens as Method Reader either way.
- Your Mac asks once more before Method Reader can see a reader you plug
  in, or open Mail or Books for you.
- The command-line converter is now method-reader. The old name, screepub, still
  works for a while and says it has been renamed.
