# Method Reader 1.2.0

**Every script you convert now waits in a Library, with a report on who speaks when, and a book of one character's part for an actor.**

## Your scripts, kept and ready

- **A Library of everything you've converted.** Open the Library tab to reopen any script's book without converting the PDF again. Rename gives a script a name its books take as their title. Remove from library asks first, then moves the script to the Trash (the Recycle Bin on Windows), so you can put it back.
- **A report for each script.** Report gives the page and scene counts and, for every character, their speeches, their words, the scenes they speak in, and where they first speak.
- **Sides, and an actor's own copy.** From the report, Sides makes a book of only the scenes one character is in. Lines in bold makes the whole script with that character's speech in bold, sung lines included. Send to e-reader puts either one on your reader.
- **You can see a save or a copy happen.** Each one shows that it's working, then where the file went.

## Fixed

- **File locations on Windows.** Some places showed a file's location in a long special form that many programs won't take when you paste it. They now show the ordinary form. The arrows that open each Settings group also draw properly.
- **A book that wouldn't open.** An invisible character in a title or a PDF's text could make a book no reader would open. Those characters are now left out.

## Good to know

- Sides count a character as in a scene when they speak in it or when its description names them, so a scene where they appear without a line is included.
