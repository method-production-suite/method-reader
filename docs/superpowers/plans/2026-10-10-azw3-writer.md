# Plan: Screepub's own AZW3 writer

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-azw3-writer-design.md`. Branch
`azw3-writer` from main 9d7fc81. Every step is test first (bun:test), one
small commit each.

## Engine

1. **Shared PalmDB container.** Move `ByteWriter`, the EXTH record shape and
   the PDB assembly out of `src/mobi/writer.ts` into `src/mobi/palmdb.ts`.
   The MOBI's bytes must not change: `tests/mobi.test.ts` pins them.
2. **`src/azw3/xml.ts`.** A small XML reader and serializer for our own
   XHTML: elements, attributes, text, the declaration, a doctype and
   comments. One serializer, so a subtree's bytes are always the bytes it
   has inside the whole document (the chunker depends on that).
3. **`src/azw3/palmdoc.ts`.** PalmDOC compression, ported from Calibre's
   `palmdoc.c`. Tests decompress with a test-side decoder and compare.
4. **`src/azw3/index.ts`.** `encint`, `toBase`, CNCX and the INDX header and
   records (`writer8/index.py`), with the four tables: skeleton, fragment
   (chunk), guide and NCX.
5. **`src/azw3/epub.ts`.** Read the EPUB zip: container, package (metadata,
   manifest, spine, cover), the nav's TOC and landmarks, stylesheets and
   pictures.
6. **`src/azw3/chunk.ts`.** The KF8 markup (`writer8/main.py` and
   `skeleton.py`): resource links to `kindle:embed`, stylesheets to
   `kindle:flow`, internal links to `kindle:pos:fid` placeholders, `aid`
   attributes, then skeletons and fragments of at most 8192 bytes, and the
   aid offset map.
7. **`src/azw3/tbs.ts`.** Trailing byte sequences for the NCX
   (`writer8/tbs.py`).
8. **`src/azw3/writer.ts`.** Text records (4096 bytes, UTF-8 overlap,
   compressed), FDST, the indices, resources, FLIS, FCIS, EOF, record 0 (the
   264-byte MOBI header, version 8) and EXTH. `buildAzw3(epubBytes)`.

## Verification

9. **`tests/helpers/kf8-reader.ts`**: a structural reader. It checks the PDB
   offsets, the MOBI header, EXTH, FDST, every INDX (counts and entries),
   trailing bytes, decompresses the text and rebuilds each file from its
   skeleton and fragments. Tests run it on field-station, images and
   torture.
10. **Calibre's reader** (skips without Calibre): `ebook-convert x.azw3
    y.epub` and `calibre-debug --inspect-mobi`, all spawns with
    `process.env` and a scratch cwd. Text, element order, TOC, metadata,
    picture count and cover.
11. **Corpus** (`/fixtures/` only): every real script converts and parses in
    Calibre's reader. Counts only, never a name.
12. **Comparison and timing**: Calibre's own AZW3 of the same EPUB, record
    and INDX counts side by side, and the time of each build. Report only.

## Wiring

13. **Ladder** (`src/export/formats.ts`): KFX when ready, else AZW3. MOBI by
    name only. `builtBy` is `'screepub'` for AZW3.
14. **Artifact** (`src/export/artifact.ts`): the AZW3 rung writes
    scratch-then-rename beside the EPUB with `buildAzw3`. No Calibre.
15. **CLI**: `export --for azw3` needs nothing installed; `--azw3 <file>` on
    a conversion, beside `--mobi` and `--fdx`; help says "(beta)".
16. **Routes and the window**: the Save a file AZW3 row is always
    available and carries the Beta badge; the KFX checklist and its summary
    stop saying Calibre is needed for AZW3; `kindleTurn` and the build-ahead
    rule follow the new `builtBy`.
17. **Calibre's AZW3 path removed**: `toAzw3`, its scratch path and its
    tests. `CALIBRE_FORMAT_GUARDS` stays for KFX and KEPUB.
